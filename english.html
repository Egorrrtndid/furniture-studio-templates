FAQ

A little clarity,
before we begin.

Can the design be customized?+

Yes. This concept is built around custom dimensions, layouts, and finishes. The final scope and available materials are agreed for each project.

How is a project priced?+

Pricing depends on dimensions, construction, materials, hardware, and installation. A tailored estimate follows a discussion of the project scope.

What should I bring to the first conversation?+

Approximate measurements, a floor plan if you have one, and a few inspiration images. An idea and a description of your space are a good starting point.
